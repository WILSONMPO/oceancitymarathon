The gathering

A coastal city, a national stride.

The Ocean City Community Marathon returns to Dar es Salaam on 14 November 2026. Hosted by Plus One Events, the third edition celebrates health, perseverance, and social cohesion — and places Tanzania on the map of world-class sports tourism, from Coco Beach to the open ocean.

The cause

Health cover for 300 orphaned children.

Proceeds and partnerships from this year’s marathon are pledged to insure 300 orphan children. The orphans are our responsibility — every bib, every cheer, every brand on the course is a step toward that cover.

Ambassadors of the road

Felix Simbu & Failuna Matanga

World Athletics marathon champion Felix Simbu and Failuna Abdi Matanga return as the faces of a race that once awarded motor vehicles to Tanzania’s 21 km champions — still unmatched in the country’s athletics story.
